Senior Insider

Ryan Mays

Founder & Senior Insider, AAA Insider

Ryan Mays is the founder and senior insider for AAA Insider, covering breaking news, transactions, league politics, organizational dysfunction and whatever else league members would prefer remain unreported.

Mays joined AAA Insider at its founding after an extensive journalism career consisting primarily of reading GroupMe messages and knowing everyone involved personally.

Known for his expansive network of league sources, Mays has cultivated relationships throughout all levels of the organization—from commissioners and former champions to managers actively fighting for their lives in the Sacko race. His reporting regularly draws upon public records, private conversations, anonymous sources, unsolicited screenshots and information obtained under circumstances AAA Insider's legal department has advised him not to elaborate upon.

Mays specializes in breaking transactions, developing league controversies and reporting information that was probably intended for a much smaller audience. His work has earned him no journalism awards.

He can be reached privately with tips. Source confidentiality is respected. Pettiness is encouraged.
