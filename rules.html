League Rules & Punishments

Existing league rules, punishments, and scoring policies.
Scoring record policy: every playoff matchup is counted once using ESPN's final matchup score. A one-week playoff matchup is eligible for the normal weekly and game leaderboards. A multi-week playoff matchup is preserved as one aggregate result and kept out of one-week scoring records so a two-week total cannot compete with a one-week score. Career W-L counts each matchup once; career scoring averages, highs and lows use one-week matchups only. multi-week playoff matchups are currently preserved in the playoff archive.
Existing league tradition

35-point defeat

A team defeated by more than 35 points is subject to a team-name and profile-picture change under the league's punishment tradition.

The records page counts qualifying losses. It does not verify that a corresponding name or picture change was completed.
