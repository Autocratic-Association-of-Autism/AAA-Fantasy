Career Standings

Historical career records and manager performance.
Scoring record policy: every playoff matchup is counted once using ESPN's final matchup score. A one-week playoff matchup is eligible for the normal weekly and game leaderboards. A multi-week playoff matchup is preserved as one aggregate result and kept out of one-week scoring records so a two-week total cannot compete with a one-week score. Career W-L counts each matchup once; career scoring averages, highs and lows use one-week matchups only. multi-week playoff matchups are currently preserved in the separate archive below.

Career standings

Official career leaderboards require at least 3 completed seasons. Completed 2026 games are included in W-L and one-week scoring records through ; finish-based metrics use completed seasons only.
Career record standard: a manager must complete 3 seasons to qualify for career averages, rates, and leaderboards. Current-season completed games count toward W-L and scoring statistics, but an unfinished season does not count toward average finish or the 3-completed-season eligibility threshold.
Limited participation — not career-record eligible
Managers with fewer than 3 completed seasons. Their games, championships, weekly scores, and season results still count in the historical archive.
