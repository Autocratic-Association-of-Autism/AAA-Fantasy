Postseason honors

Every manager earns a postseason legacy tier based on their best completed achievements: championships, title-game appearances, third-place finishes, and playoff qualifications. Stars represent additional achievements within the current tier; promotion starts a new tier without inherited stars. Playoff appearances count completed top-six championship-bracket qualifications, not consolation appearances. Sacko finishes are tracked separately and never reduce a manager’s rank. The in-progress 2026 season does not contribute to postseason honors.

Sacko Bracket

The Sacko is awarded to the loser of the consolation bracket, not automatically the last-place regular-season team. The league has confirmed the 2021–22 through 2025–26 recipients and punishments.
Punishment history for the 2021–22 through 2025–26 seasons is confirmed by the league. The Sacko goes to the consolation-bracket loser. The 2026–27 punishment has not yet been recorded.
