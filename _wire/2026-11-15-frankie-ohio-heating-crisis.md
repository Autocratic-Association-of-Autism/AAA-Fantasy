AAA Insider has received troubling information regarding the living conditions of longtime league member Frankie Yentz.

**Frankie reportedly does not have functioning heat in his house.**

The revelation has shocked absolutely nobody familiar with the state of Ohio.

While the rest of America prepares for winter by servicing furnaces and adjusting thermostats, Frankie is apparently entering the annual Ohio tradition of discovering which household possessions can be used as insulation.

The newsroom initially considered this an isolated household maintenance issue.

Then we remembered where Frankie lives.

### A humanitarian crisis in the Midwest

Ohio has long occupied an unfortunate position in American society: geographically located within the United States, yet seemingly excluded from many of its benefits.

Frankie's reported lack of heating is merely the latest reminder of the challenges facing residents of this struggling territory.

Elsewhere, Americans enjoy modern conveniences such as central heating, functioning infrastructure, and the opportunity to leave Ohio.

Frankie apparently has none of those luxuries.

The newsroom is particularly concerned that, with temperatures dropping, Frankie may soon be forced to burn something valuable for warmth.

Possibilities include furniture, discarded waiver claims, or the remaining goodwill he earned during his recent trade negotiations.

### A remarkable reversal of priorities

The timing is particularly unfortunate.

Just last week, Frankie astonished the league by discovering that his telephone could make outgoing calls.

He contacted managers individually, pursued the commissioner, and conducted an extensive lobbying campaign to defend his trade with Glen.

Apparently, Frankie has enough energy to operate an entire political outreach program, but not enough heat to keep his house warm.

Priorities are important.

The good news is that Frankie has already demonstrated considerable experience surviving in hostile environments.

He has spent years living in Ohio.

A cold house should be manageable.

The newsroom is considering a charitable initiative to help Frankie through the winter, although there are concerns that any money raised would immediately be redirected toward fantasy football dues or another questionable transaction with Glen.

### AAA offers its support

We encourage league members to keep Frankie in their thoughts as he approaches another difficult winter.

For now, the most important thing is ensuring that Frankie remains safe and comfortable.

Preferably without requiring anyone to move to Ohio to assist him.
