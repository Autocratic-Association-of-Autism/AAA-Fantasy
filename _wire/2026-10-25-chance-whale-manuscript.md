*An explosive anonymous tip reveals an alleged unpublished novel involving maritime surgery, an unusually named protagonist, and a sailor whose medical condition has raised serious questions.*

At 1:49 Sunday morning, while most of the AAA slept peacefully, the newsroom received a disturbing literary discovery.

A trusted confidential source operating under the name **Captain Crack Dinero** submitted what appears to be the opening chapter of an unpublished novel attributed to commissioner candidate **Chance Robinson**.

The source reports obtaining the document from Robinson's unattended laptop. The manuscript introduces readers to **Perchance**, a female whaling captain whose professional specialty is described in two words:

**"Whale circumcisions."**

Yes. Whale circumcisions.

Perchance commands a vessel known as *The Snippety-Ship*, which reportedly returns to port carrying enough marine foreskin to create serious logistical concerns for the international shipping industry.

But the manuscript's most troubling development involves a sailor named **Status Ticks**, whose unusual physiological responses to the captain's surgical activities create what the author describes, through one character, as:

**"A medical condition! A very specific medical condition!"**

AAA Insider's medical department has declined to elaborate.

The newsroom's literary analysts were particularly struck by the author's willingness to explore complex themes of maritime commerce, involuntary arousal, and the practical difficulties of operating a surgical instrument on an exceptionally large mammal.

They were considerably less impressed by the absence of a coherent plot.

## CANDIDATE BREAKS SILENCE

Following the submission, AAA Insider publicly approached Robinson in GroupMe for comment.

Faced with questions surrounding the alleged manuscript, its suspiciously familiar protagonist, and an alarming quantity of whale genitalia, the commissioner candidate issued the following statement:

**"Geez I wonder who the author is"**

That was his entire response.

Not an explanation. Not a categorical denial. Not even a statement expressing concern for the whales.

Instead, Robinson appeared interested in establishing the identity of an author—although whether he meant the manuscript's author or the anonymous contributor remains unclear.

AAA Insider has referred the ambiguity to its legal department, which has requested that the newsroom stop sending it pictures of whales.

## A CANDIDATE WITH TWO VERY DIFFERENT VISIONS

Robinson has spent recent weeks campaigning on promises of sweeping reform, bigger prizes, and a brighter future for the AAA.

His alleged literary pursuits suggest an equally ambitious vision for the maritime industry.

The campaign has yet to explain how Robinson finds time to pursue public office while apparently developing what could become the most sexually confusing nautical epic since the invention of the harpoon.

More concerningly, **the manuscript contains substantially more detail about whale genitalia than Robinson's campaign has provided about funding increased league prizes.**

When given an opportunity to clarify matters, the candidate instead introduced another mystery.
