Saturday's campaign news began with two prospective league members appearing sympathetic to **Chance Robinson's movement**. It took one display of decisive leadership from the existing administration to introduce serious doubts about where their loyalties might ultimately land.

Co-commissioner **Ryan Mays**, responding to efforts to bring Chance-aligned applicants into the AAA, announced a **“TOTAL AND COMPLETE SHUTDOWN”** pending extreme vetting. His comprehensive border-security framework also called for a tremendous wall, with Chance responsible for the bill. Campaign finance officials were advised that friendship might be accepted as payment.

The announcement made an immediate impression on prospective recruits **Josh and Brandon**.

**“Very Democratic of you Mays,”** Josh observed. AAA Insider has entered the remark into the official record of favorable assessments of the co-commissioner's leadership.

Brandon went further, reconsidering the political arrangement in real time:

**“Damn. Maybe Ryan should be the new commish. Sorry Chance.”**

He then identified precisely the quality that had won his attention:

**“IM DRAWN TO SWIFT DECISION MAKING WITHOUT ASKING ANYONE ANYTHING UNDER THE GUISE OF DEMOCRACY.”**

Those are strong words from a potential new member. The newsroom is not prepared to ignore such a glowing appraisal simply because it arrived during a contentious debate over admission.

Incumbent commissioner **Casey Hutchins** reinforced confidence in the current leadership structure: **“Mays is co-commish for a reason. He holds power as do i. We run this league effectively.”**

Josh subsequently described the developments as a **“Damning point against the Chance campaign”** and invited Chance to defend his position. No response from Chance appears in the supplied exchange.

AAA Insider is also examining the possibility that [Friday night's anonymous expansion manifesto]({{ '/wire/2026-10-23-anonymous-expansion-email.html' | relative_url }}) originated from within the prospective applicant pool. **The author remains unidentified, and there is no verified evidence linking either applicant to the email.**

If confirmed, it would represent a remarkable political journey: anonymously lobbying against the existing establishment on Friday, then praising its leadership by Saturday morning. The writer's identity is still a mystery. **The speed of the potential political realignment, however, is a matter of public record.**

To be clear, **neither prospective member has formally endorsed Casey**, neither has joined the league, and a possible move to 12 teams remains subject to the end-of-season vote Casey previously described. The political movement, however, is now worth watching.

**AAA Insider finding:** Chance entered the expansion debate expecting potential allies. By the end of it, one was floating Ryan for commissioner and another was praising the administration's democratic credentials. The vetting process appears to be producing results.
