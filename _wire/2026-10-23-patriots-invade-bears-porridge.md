Ryan Hutchins entered Thursday's Bears–Patriots watch party with a prediction, a gambling argument, and the certainty of a man who had not yet seen the second half.

The first difficulty was arithmetic. While interrogating Ryan's expected $50 profit on a Bears wager, Hutchins edited his own message about a possible Patriots bet and eventually announced:

**“I also cannot do math apparently today.”**

An encouraging start for a man offering live financial oversight.

Later, as the game unfolded, Hutchins assured the room: **“It's totally going to be Patriots ball game after the half. Those Bears look gassed.”**

He then provided the tactical analysis the NFL has been missing: the Patriots were supposedly going to enter the Bears' house, eat their porridge, sit in their chairs, sleep in their beds, **“And then win.”**

When challenged, Hutchins clarified that this was specifically a prediction about *after halftime*. In an important development, that was the portion of the contest everyone was already discussing.

By night's end, Ryan was celebrating a Bears victory and counting claimed winnings that included **$50 from Hutchins**. The newsroom has not inspected anyone's Venmo records and cannot certify that payment was actually made.

Glen, meanwhile, contributed **“FUCK THE BEARS!!!”** to the broadcast discussion, the sort of measured football analysis that normally requires a press credential.

**AAA Insider finding:** The Patriots were apparently due to win once the Bears were finished winning. The schedule never accommodated this request.
