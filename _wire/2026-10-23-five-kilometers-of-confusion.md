The AAA has spent years proving that last place carries consequences. On Thursday, it began planning one that may require the entire league to pass elementary-school mathematics first.

The proposed punishment: the last-place manager runs a **5K** while everyone else drinks at a bar on the loser's tab. The league also discussed a costume, a ride to the starting line, and the possibility of holding the event around the Super Bowl or the next draft.

A healthy discussion about logistics followed. By healthy, we mean **the league could not agree what a kilometer was**.

Chance Robinson assured the group that a 5K was **“like 3.6 miles.”** Another participant briefly argued for a 3K. Someone thought the runner needed to go five miles. A route involving 2.5 miles *out* and 2.5 miles *back* also entered consideration.

For the record, five kilometers is approximately **3.1 miles**. The newsroom has submitted this finding for peer review, which is an unnecessarily dangerous thing to do around these particular peers.

A manager posting as **“The real Chris turner”** announced ambitions of finishing in under 20 minutes. Others expressed interest in the more traditional spectator disciplines of drinking and heckling. Ryan Hutchins proposed using **Chance's fundraiser money to buy a furry costume** for the loser, which is either campaign-finance innovation or the beginning of a very troubling ethics inquiry.

No final route, costume, or date is established by the conversation. At present, the most complete part of the punishment is the plan to have somebody else pay for alcohol.

**AAA Insider finding:** The league has designed a punishment requiring physical endurance, fiscal responsibility, and the ability to convert kilometers into miles. It has demonstrated confidence in exactly none of them.
