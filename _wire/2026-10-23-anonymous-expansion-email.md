The document accuses **Larry Terrell and Frankie Yentz** of opposing a move from 10 to 12 teams, describing their alleged stance as **“a suspicious lack of ball knowledge disguised as league governance.”** It repeatedly demands to know what the two men are afraid of.

There is, however, an administrative complication: **nobody has formally proposed expanding the league.** The idea has been discussed informally, and the newsroom has not independently verified that Larry or Frankie opposes it. At present, the anonymous author has successfully started a fight with two men over a vote that does not exist.

The tip then pivots from expansion to commissioner politics, questions Casey Hutchins's tenure, and prominently advertises **#ChanceForChange**. Chance supports expansion. Casey's position remains unclear. The sender's political sympathies appear somewhat less difficult to determine, though the newsroom has no evidence of any formal campaign connection.

The email also closes by informing AAA Insider that its newsroom **“will be watching Larry and Frankie very closely.”** This is a bold assignment from someone who has not provided a name, a formal proposal, or a verified statement from either alleged opponent.

Larry, Frankie, and Casey are welcome to put their actual positions on the record. Until then, the accusations remain the anonymous sender's, not AAA Insider's findings.

**AAA Insider finding:** The opposition is allegedly panicking, the expansion has not been proposed, and the only thing currently growing is the tip line's word count.
