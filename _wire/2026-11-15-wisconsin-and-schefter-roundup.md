### Glen Hutchins Proposes Removing Wisconsin From the United States

Glen's hatred of the Green Bay Packers has reached a point where defeating them on the football field is no longer sufficient.

He now supports removing Wisconsin from the country entirely.

"I wouldn’t mind going to 49 states or letting Puerto Rico take Wisconsins place to keep it 50," Glen announced.

He subsequently proposed the creation of the Puerto Rican Packers.

The newsroom understands Glen's frustration, but notes that redrawing the nation's borders whenever his football team disappoints him may prove administratively challenging.
