Three weeks are officially in the books, which is enough football to identify trends, make sweeping conclusions, and quietly delete those conclusions when they become inconvenient. Njigba please is undefeated. The Queen Mother is not. Coke(r) Caine has scored like a contender and somehow has a losing record. Lamborghini Murci has discovered the strategic benefits of playing opponents who occasionally forget to score. Ryan has already experienced both a perfectly normal fantasy team and whatever happened in Week 2.

## The Draft Audit

It’s our year opened with Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Derrick Henry and Josh Allen, which has worked exactly as annoyingly well as expected. JSN and Allen immediately became two of the highest-scoring players in the league, while Henry has done Derrick Henry things.

Coke(r) Caine landed Jahmyr Gibbs first overall and somehow found Bryce Young with the final pick of the draft. Champ may have landed the best late value in Brock Purdy at pick 104. Lamborghini Murci came away with CeeDee Lamb, James Cook, Garrett Wilson, Travis Etienne and Tee Higgins. On paper: irritatingly competent. This concludes the complimentary portion of Lamborghini Murci coverage.

The Cukings built around Ja’Marr Chase, Kenneth Walker, Drake London, Breece Hall and Jalen Hurts. Weso-Hornay got Christian McCaffrey, Chris Olave and Jayden Daniels. la porta potty opened with Justin Jefferson and De’Von Achane before adding Lamar Jackson and Sam LaPorta. Coach has gotten real production from Amon-Ra St. Brown, while Kyle Pitts has mostly functioned as a tax deduction. The Queen Mother started with Puka Nacua, Saquon Barkley and Nico Collins, a trio that should have produced considerably more happiness than 0–3.

Ryan took Bijan Robinson at No. 2 and Patrick Mahomes at pick 119. Mahomes looked like unnecessary quarterback depth until Caleb Williams got hurt, at which point it became the type of insurance policy that can be retroactively classified as foresight. Ryan also selected Colston Loveland at pick 39. Loveland has 8.4 points. Every draft has one selection future historians agree not to discuss.

## Week 1: Everyone Is a Genius

It’s our year opened with a 150.06–136.66 win over Champ in one of the best matchups of the week. Coke(r) Caine posted the week's high score at 162.50, somehow while leaving Jalen Coker’s 33.8 points on the bench. Scoring 162 while voluntarily ignoring another 34 is unnecessary behavior.

la porta potty edged The Queen Mother 119.16–115.30. Lamborghini Murci defeated Coach 114.60–86.26, heroically overcoming the grueling challenge of an opponent scoring 86 points.

Ryan lost 126.02–120.16 to The Cukings, but spent the afternoon in Charlotte cheering for the Bears while Caleb Williams put up 37.26 fantasy points. Carolina's defense provided excellent hospitality to the visiting Bears. Fantasy result: loss. Actual football experience: considerably better.

## Week 2: The Incident

Ryan scored **55.62**. There is no tasteful way to frame it. Caleb got hurt. Bijan scored 11.1. Jeremiyah Love scored 7.5. Jameson Williams scored 5.3. Loveland continued his campaign against the concept of fantasy points.

**Bench damage:** Mahomes 28.98 · Panthers D/ST 24.0 · Diggs 21.7

Lamborghini Murci won 143.80–55.62 and celebrated accordingly. For historical accuracy, he needed 55.63 points to win. The other 88.17 were decorative.

Elsewhere, Champ erupted for 162.28, It’s our year scored another 156.52, Coach rebounded, and Weso-Hornay collected its first win.

## Week 3: The Market Corrects

Ryan responded with 132.24 and beat Coach 132.24–114.56. Bijan scored 35.3, Jeremiyah Love added 21.9, and Mahomes handled quarterback duties. Ryan also left Luther Burden’s 19.8 points on the bench, proving Bears production occasionally exceeds available lineup capacity.

Njigba please — the artist formerly known as It’s our year — moved to 3–0. Kenneth Skywalker edged Coke(r) Caine 127.22–124.24. Weso-Hornay improved to 2–1 after its second straight opponent failed to reach 100.

Then Champ beat Lamborghini Murci 138.98–119.52. One week after Lamborghini Murci spent considerable energy celebrating Ryan’s fantasy corpse, he lost to his own brother. Some stories write themselves.

### Bears Department

With Caleb Williams unavailable, Chicago still handled Philadelphia 27–7 behind third-string quarterback Case Keenum. Luther Burden scored for the Bears and also produced useful fantasy points for Ryan's bench, because apparently Bears excellence has become difficult to contain.

## The Transaction Desk

Macon and Glen completed the league’s only trade so far: Coke(r) Caine received Dallas Goedert, while Champ received Brock Bowers.

**Coke(r) Caine received:** Dallas Goedert — 23.7 in Week 1, 25.1 total through Week 3  
**Champ received:** Brock Bowers — 27.6 in Week 3, 27.6 total through Week 3

Goedert won the opening round. Bowers answered with 27.6 in Week 3 and moved ahead in total points. Current verdict: slight early edge to Champ because Bowers has the more interesting long-term profile. Calling it a robbery would be premature. Bowers' breakout also came during Champ's win over Lamborghini Murci, so the trade receives bonus points for community service.
