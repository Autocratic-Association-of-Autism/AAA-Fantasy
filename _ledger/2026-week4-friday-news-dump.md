*By Ryan Mays — AAA Insider*

There are weeks when a fantasy-football newsroom simply reports the news.

This was not one of those weeks.

Since Tuesday, AAA Insider launched a Tip Line, became the subject of multiple allegations, discovered that the commissioner is apparently involved in a sexual relationship with her own husband, learned that Chance may be filled with fruit punch, published an exhaustive profile of Zak Wesolek for reasons absolutely unrelated to the Week 4 schedule, and watched Ryan Hutchins discover that historical statistics are considerably less enjoyable when other people know how to use them.

It has been a productive 48 hours.

Welcome to **The Friday News Dump**, AAA Insider's recurring collection of everything that happened since Tuesday that deserved attention, investigation or, in several cases, considerably less of both.

## The Newsroom Has Become the News

AAA Insider would prefer to begin this publication with somebody else's problems.

Unfortunately, the league had other ideas.

Earlier this week, the newsroom unveiled its official Insider profile featuring founder and senior insider Ryan Mays.

Almost immediately, questions emerged regarding unidentified figures appearing in the artwork.

An unidentified child.

An unidentified cat.

Potentially additional domestic arrangements.

A source submitting under the name **Bob Saget** used the first formal Tip Line submission to question whether Mays maintains a secret second family, a second wife and, perhaps most damagingly, a previously undisclosed allegiance to cats.

The submission threatened possible escalation to *The New York Times*.

AAA Insider initially declined to dignify the allegations.

Then the GroupMe found them.

Ryan Hutchins subsequently circulated what he described as evidence and declared:

> “Ryan Mays is not the man we think he is.”

Casey separately identified a suspicious child in the imagery and suggested that Chucky may also be involved.

At this point, an internal review became unavoidable.

AAA Insider has therefore opened an independent investigation into AAA Insider, conducted by AAA Insider.

Early findings indicate that the newsroom has found no reason to question the integrity of the newsroom.

The unidentified cat remains unexplained.

Legal counsel has requested that readers stop asking follow-up questions.

## Ryan Hutchins Discovers the Dangers of Open Records

The league's new historical archive was designed to preserve six years of fantasy-football history.

Nobody anticipated that Chance would immediately use it as a weapon.

On Thursday, Chance discovered that **Ryan Hutchins owns five of the ten lowest weekly scores currently preserved in league history**.

Five.

Of ten.

That is not appearing on a leaderboard.

That is establishing a branch office.

Hutchins responded:

> “I may cover that leaderboard, but I am not at the top.”

AAA Insider has reviewed this defense.

It is technically correct in the same way a man standing waist-deep in sewage may accurately note that somebody nearby is standing in it up to his shoulders.

The newsroom will continue examining Hutchins' historical relationship with catastrophic scoring performances, two Sackos and his increasingly public determination to avoid a third.

There is growing evidence that Sacko avoidance is no longer merely a fantasy-football objective for Hutchins.

It may be a governing philosophy.

## AAA Insider Published a Completely Normal Profile of Zak Wesolek

Earlier Friday, AAA Insider debuted **Meet the League**, an occasional profile series intended to introduce readers to the personalities behind the rosters.

By extraordinary coincidence, the inaugural subject was **Zak Wesolek**, who happens to be playing Ryan Mays this weekend.

The newsroom reviewed Zak's pole-vaulting career, employment as a travel nurse, remarkable lack of fantasy trades, 2024 Sacko campaign, 2025 championship appearance and documented willingness to disappear from GroupMe for extended periods of time.

AAA Insider strongly rejects suggestions that selecting Ryan's current opponent as the first subject represents editorial bias.

It represents **timing**.

There is a difference.

Probably.

## From the Tip Line

AAA Insider promised that every submitted tip would receive a response.

This was perhaps an administrative mistake.

Nevertheless, a promise is a promise.

### Tip #001 — The Cat, the Child and the Alleged Second Family
**Submitted as:** Bob Saget  
**Source status:** May be named publicly

The source raised questions regarding an unidentified child, an unidentified cat, a possible second wife and the possibility that AAA Insider founder Ryan Mays secretly maintains an entirely separate household.

**AAA Insider response:**

The allegations have been referred to an independent internal investigation being conducted by AAA Insider into AAA Insider.

The newsroom categorically rejects the implication that mere possession of a cat would constitute journalistic misconduct.

Everything else remains under review.

**Status:** Investigation ongoing. Cat unidentified.

### Tip #002 — Commissioner and Participant
**Submitted as:** Anonymous  
**Source status:** Anonymous

An anonymous source alleged that Commissioner Casey Hutchins is sleeping with a league member.

AAA Insider immediately reviewed the available evidence.

The source did not identify the league member. The newsroom can, however, confirm that Casey is married to league member **Ryan Hutchins**, and the couple is expecting a child in November.

AAA Insider contacted the commissioner for comment.

She responded:

> “No comment.”

A disciplined answer from an experienced executive who understands that not every anonymous allegation deserves oxygen.

After reviewing the available evidence, AAA Insider found no indication of corruption, improper favoritism or misconduct beyond the commissioner apparently maintaining a relationship with her own husband.

Critics may call this a conflict of interest.

AAA Insider calls it **traditional family values and continuity of government**.

**Status:** Scandal not found. Ryan Hutchins remains implicated.

### Tip #003 — Commissioner Scandal
**Submitted as:** Anonymous  
**Source status:** Anonymous

A separate anonymous submission alleged that Commissioner Casey Hutchins directed an offensive self-harm remark at another person.

AAA Insider requested comment.

The commissioner responded:

> “No comment. (But they probably deserved it)”

The newsroom has received no evidence establishing that the alleged incident occurred.

The commissioner's response, however, has been independently verified.

Some publications might characterize this as poor crisis communication.

AAA Insider sees an executive unwilling to prejudge an unverified allegation while simultaneously maintaining the possibility that the hypothetical complainant may have been extraordinarily annoying.

That is not recklessness.

That is **keeping all options open**.

**Status:** Allegation unverified. Commissioner decisive. Government functioning normally.

### Tip #004 — Chance May Be a Capri-Sun
**Submitted as:** Respect Da Pouch  
**Source status:** May be named publicly

The Tip Line received the following information:

> “If you poke a straw into Chance's belly, fruit punch comes out.”

AAA Insider has been unable to independently verify Chance Robinson's internal beverage composition.

The newsroom also lacks the medical authority to conduct invasive pouch testing.

However, after reviewing the allegation, the claim has not been dismissed outright.

**Status:** Monitoring. Straw not yet authorized.

### Tip #005 — The Boy Scouts Submission
**Submitted as:** Louis Swanson  
**Source status:** May be named publicly

AAA Insider received a lengthy submission purporting to describe an incident involving “Louis Swanson” while he was a minor.

The newsroom has no evidence that Louis submitted it, no evidence the described event occurred and no intention of converting an unverifiable story involving a minor into league lore simply because somebody found the Tip Line at 4:19 in the morning.

AAA Insider promised to acknowledge every tip.

It did not promise to believe them.

**Status:** Rejected as reporting.

The Tip Line remains open.

Unfortunately.

### Tip #006 — Frankenstein Has Entered the Chat
**Submitted as:** Dr. Frankie  
**Source status:** May be named publicly

The Tip Line received what appears to be either a literary warning, a confession or the opening paragraph of an extremely low-budget horror movie:

> “Why did you make me?”  
> The monster cried.  
> “You gave me a soul,  
> Then left me outside.”  
> Ryan kept running,  
> But nowhere was far—  
> You can’t outrun something  
> You’ve made who you are.

AAA Insider has reviewed the submission.

The newsroom does not currently understand it.

Given the name “Dr. Frankie,” the monster imagery and the repeated references to Ryan creating something he can no longer escape, our working theory is that **Ryan has somehow become Dr. Frankenstein in a metaphor nobody bothered to explain before filing it with the press.**

**Status:** Meaning unclear. Threat level literary.

The Tip Line remains open.

Unfortunately.

## Transaction Desk: Some People Are Actually Managing Their Teams

Amid the investigations, scandal and pouch-related medical questions, several managers quietly continued playing fantasy football.

Ryan added **Braelon Allen, Darren Waller and Cleveland's defense** during the week.

Chance added **J.K. Dobbins, Tyreek Hill and Najee Harris**, continuing his longstanding philosophy that a roster spot left unused for more than six consecutive minutes constitutes organizational negligence.

Casey added **Keenan Allen, Jordan Addison and Buffalo's defense**, demonstrating that the commissioner can simultaneously manage league government, navigate scandal and operate the waiver wire.

Zak, meanwhile, had made essentially no meaningful changes beyond those forced upon him by injury.

AAA Insider already published approximately 1,500 words regarding this behavior.

No further comment is necessary.

Except for Zak.

## Things AAA Insider Is Monitoring

Commissioner-election season is beginning to produce subtle signs of life.

Casey has indicated privately that she intends to remain involved in league leadership, and AAA Insider has separately confirmed that Ryan Mays remains both the league's original commissioner and Casey's current co-commissioner.

This creates what journalism schools may refer to as a “conflict of interest.”

Fortunately, nobody involved attended journalism school.

Elsewhere, Frankie's continued relationship with league communications remains under observation after he recently appeared unaware that AAA Insider existed until a link was personally texted to him.

AAA Insider does not currently classify failing to read the GroupMe as a punishable offense.

The newsroom is reviewing whether it should be.

## The Week in One Sentence

AAA Insider launched a Tip Line and within approximately 48 hours learned that its founder may have a second family, the commissioner sleeps with her husband, Chance may contain juice, Ryan Hutchins owns half of an undesirable leaderboard and at least one league member believes the Tip Line should have absolutely no editorial standards.

**The system is working.**
