*By Ryan Mays — AAA Insider*

Thursday has already put a few points on the board, but most of Week 4 is still waiting to happen. Waivers are mostly settled, lineups are mostly set, and everyone has had enough time to make responsible decisions.

Naturally, that has not happened.

Here is where all five matchups stand before Sunday turns several people's weekends into evidence.

## BIJAN MUSTARD (1–2) vs. WESO-HORNAY (2–1)
### *The profile subject gets an immediate chance to file a rebuttal*

Ryan enters Sunday up **8.0–3.5** after Cleveland's defense outscored Michael Pittman Jr. on Thursday. ESPN still gives Ryan a modest edge, but this matchup is very much alive.

Zak's biggest problem is Tyler Shough at quarterback with Jayden Daniels out. That means Christian McCaffrey, Chris Olave and Rashee Rice need to do more than usual, which is a tough way to spend a weekend after being publicly profiled by the league's media apparatus.

Ryan has Mahomes, Bijan, Skattebo, Diggs and Waller carrying the load. Denzel Boston already scored 12.9 on Ryan's bench Thursday, so the newsroom has also contributed one completely unnecessary self-inflicted wound.

The history here is subtle only if you consider an **84.46-point win** and a **78.58-point revenge beating** subtle. Ryan destroyed Zak in 2023. Zak returned the favor in 2025. Apparently these two treat mercy like an optional scoring category.

### Keys to Victory
**Ryan:** Get a normal Mahomes-and-Bijan week and do not let Christian McCaffrey turn this into another one of those Ryan–Zak games where somebody gets publicly dismembered.  
**Zak:** Get enough competence from Tyler Shough that the rest of the roster is not forced to perform CPR by halftime.

### Bold Prediction
McCaffrey gives Ryan at least one genuine scare, but Zak eventually reaches the part of the afternoon where having Tyler Shough at quarterback becomes less of a roster decision and more of a medical condition.

### Ryan's Pick
**Bijan Mustard.** After a thorough internal review, AAA Insider has found Ryan Mays to be both qualified and handsome.

## COKE(R) CAINE (1–2) vs. CHANCEFORCHANGE2027 (0–3)
### *The commissioner is busy demonstrating family values while the roster awaits further guidance*

Casey is 0–3, which is technically bad. Fortunately, fantasy standings are not the only measure of executive performance.

As this is being written, Casey and Ryan Hutchins are at their baby shower preparing for the arrival of their child in November. So while everyone else is staring at questionable tags and pretending they understand coach-speak, the commissioner is handling league government, campaign season, pregnancy and an actual family milestone.

That is called leadership under pressure.

Casey's roster still has plenty of talent with Puka, Saquon, Swift, Kittle, Aaron Jones and Stafford. Macon is only 1–2, but his scoring profile has been better than the record, and Pittsburgh's defense already gave him five points Thursday.

A Casey win would be useful because it would allow the campaign to immediately rebrand 1–3 as “momentum.” AAA Insider has no objection to this. Messaging matters.

### Keys to Victory
**Macon:** Let Gibbs and Kyren do the work and avoid becoming the first victim of a commissioner bounce-back narrative.  
**Casey:** Get normal production from the stars and remind the league that incumbency comes with advantages.

### Bold Prediction
Casey wins, and by Sunday night the phrase “turning point” appears somewhere in campaign-adjacent communications.

### Ryan's Pick
**ChanceforChange2027.** Stability. Family. Governance. Touchdowns. In that order.

## KENNETH SKYWALKER (2–1) vs. LAMBORGHINI MURCI (2–1)
### *The lineup screen remains available at no additional charge*

Ryan Hutchins' lineup looks absurd, but this is not an accident.

He is **deliberately** leaving players on the bench, and everyone in the league knows it.

Harold Fannin Jr. gave him **11.7 points** Thursday. DK Metcalf scored **16.5 on the bench**, so Hutchins has already willingly watched more points disappear than most managers would tolerate before opening ESPN in a cold sweat.

Larry, meanwhile, has Ja'Marr Chase, Kenneth Walker III, Drake London, David Montgomery and Trevor Lawrence in active lineup slots, choosing the comparatively traditional strategy of attempting to score as many points as possible.

This comes in the same week Hutchins was reminded that he owns **five of the ten lowest weekly scores in league history** and after publicly telling Larry he would “sabotage” his team to avoid a third Sacko.

That quote has aged beautifully.

### Keys to Victory
**Larry:** Punish whatever the hell Hutchins is doing before it somehow turns into strategy.  
**Hutchins:** Make the deliberate benching look calculated instead of deranged.

### Bold Prediction
Hutchins sticks with the bit long enough to make the matchup unnecessarily strange, and Metcalf's 16.5 bench points become part of the postgame argument no matter who wins.

### Ryan's Pick
**Kenneth Skywalker.** Larry currently holds a commanding edge in the category of “players scheduled to count.”

## LA PORTA POTTY (1–2) vs. NJIGBA PLEASE (3–0)
### *Chance continues to treat waivers like a hostage negotiation*

Chance is the league's only undefeated manager and has responded to 3–0 by behaving like his roster is somehow in crisis.

This week he added J.K. Dobbins, Tyreek Hill, Najee Harris and Washington's defense. Louis dropped Dobbins, and Chance scooped him up almost immediately, the fantasy equivalent of following someone out of a restaurant and taking the chair before it gets cold.

Louis has the harder problem. Justin Jefferson and DeVonta Smith are out, and Lamar Jackson is being asked to perform his usual role of “please solve everything.”

Chance's core of Josh Allen, Derrick Henry, Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Zay Flowers gives him the cleaner path, and this is before factoring in the possibility that fruit punch actually does come out of him when punctured.

### Keys to Victory
**Chance:** Avoid overmanaging a team that is already 3–0.  
**Louis:** Hope Lamar Jackson accidentally becomes all eleven offensive players.

### Bold Prediction
Chance gets to 4–0 and begins speaking about the season in documentary voiceover.

### Ryan's Pick
**Njigba Please.** I considered contrarianism, then remembered Louis is starting from a hospital waiting room.

## COACH (1–2) vs. CHAMP (2–1)
### *Frankie has finally chosen a communication method: points*

Frankie got the best Thursday start of the week when Quinshon Judkins scored **21.6 points**, which is more information than Frankie has contributed to GroupMe in several calendar years.

Glen still has Jonathan Taylor, Ashton Jeanty, Trey McBride, Davante Adams, Brock Bowers and Brock Purdy, so this is probably the best actual fantasy matchup of the week.

He also has **15.6 Jaylen Warren points on the bench**, which are useless now but potentially priceless later if Glen loses by 14.

Frankie has Amon-Ra, Chase Brown, Pickens, Kelce and Goff still to go. He also may or may not have submitted a cryptic “Dr. Frankie” Frankenstein poem to the Tip Line.

AAA Insider still does not know what the poem means.

We are increasingly open to the theory that neither did the author.

### Keys to Victory
**Frankie:** Keep converting silence into points.  
**Glen:** Have the stars erase Thursday and never look at Warren's score again.

### Bold Prediction
This finishes close enough that Glen mentions Warren's bench points while insisting they did not matter.

### Ryan's Pick
**Coach.** Glen losing creates more usable journalism, and AAA Insider supports renewable resources.
