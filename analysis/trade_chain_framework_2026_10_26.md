# AAA 2026 two-step / multi-team trade framework

As of ESPN export 2026-10-27 01:01:56 UTC (league 1438350867). This is a scouting framework, NOT a list of accepted trades or independently validated probabilities.

## Objective
Find paths User -> Manager A -> Manager B (possibly third buyer C), including acquisition of a liquid intermediate player. Optimize **net expected points from every week's legal lineup**, projected standings/playoff and regular-season-last-place sensitivity, league-specific byes, opponent improvement and roster spot/bye coverage. Do not maximize nominal trade-chart points. Prefer a direct trade with the same outcome.

## Rules
1. Protect JSN, Luther Burden, Colston Loveland absent a truly exceptional complete deal. A Bears tie-break preference favors useful CHI players only where projected fantasy value is competitive.
2. **Second-trade failure:** intermediate acquisition must be acceptable by itself for Week 8 and the rest of the season. Reject if Week 8 starters lose expected points beyond a robust improvement interval or RB depth collapses.
3. **Two credible exits:** before obtaining any bridge player, identify at least two owners with positional needs AND comparable value to return. A roster need is not an offer or proof of willingness. Obtain informal indications first, and do not present verbal interest as binding. Model probability of failed second trade conservatively; no invented percentages.
4. Use multiple published ROS trade charts as sanity checks; their scales differ. Account for 2-for-1 premium, roster cap and forced drops.
5. Check exact Week 10 (CHI/DEN/PHI/TB), Week 11 (ATL/CLE/GB/LAR/NE/SEA) and Week 13 (BAL/IND/LV/NYJ) byes, and league-specific trade deadline/review processing. A +1 point weekly upgrade that causes a crucial bye shortage can be a net loss.
6. Current FA market includes Aaron Rodgers, Justin Herbert, Sam Darnold, Baker Mayfield and TEs Harold Fannin Jr., Jake Ferguson, Brenton Strange; inexpensive QB and TE bridges are low-liquidity. Prefer WR bridges with genuine starting value.
